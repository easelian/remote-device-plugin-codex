# Remote Device Hub: small-size icon revision

Date: 2026-09-29. Created with the built-in ImageGen tool, based on the previous icon.

The old terminal glyph occupied too little of a 16–24 px plugin icon. Its orbit,
satellite nodes and terminal frame competed with the main symbol. The revision
uses an oversized white chevron and cyan underscore on navy, with thicker strokes
and no surrounding details. Generated artwork was exported mechanically with
macOS sips to 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 256 and 512 px; no programmatic design replacement.

Measured on the 256 px export, representative solid foreground/background pixels
have approximately 18.0:1 white/navy and 13.6:1 cyan/navy luminance contrast. These
measurements exclude antialiased edges and are not a blanket accessibility certification.
The 16 px and 24 px files were inspected at their native size.

## ImageGen prompt

Use case: logo-brand, edit/replacement. Edit the supplied Remote Device Hub icon to fix poor readability at 16×16 and 20×20 in a ChatGPT plugin list. Keep its deep navy and bright white/cyan brand palette and terminal-command meaning. Radically simplify to ONE oversized terminal prompt symbol: a very bold pure-white right-pointing chevron `>` on the left and a thick bright-cyan underscore `_` on the right. Eliminate the elliptical orbit, all satellite nodes and the outlined terminal window entirely. No thin lines or small details. Background solid uniform deep navy #0B1728, full-bleed square with no external margins. The combined `>_` symbol must fill about 78% of canvas width and 58% of its height, optically centered. Chevron and underscore stroke thickness about 12% of canvas width, with subtly rounded ends; keep a clear gap so the two elements stay distinct at 16 pixels. White #FFFFFF, cyan #55F5E6. Only these flat solid colors, crisp edges, no gradients, no shadows, no glow, no texture, no mockup, no letters or brand name, no surrounding frame. Deliver a single square production icon, opaque background. Small-scale clarity is more important than decorative detail.
